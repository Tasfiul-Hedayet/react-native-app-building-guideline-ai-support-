# How to upload your apk file to apkpure/uptodown 

* unique package name. e.g - .com.[dev_name]/[company_name].project_name
* app name - general app name
* sign your apk with unique keystrokes (and keep them)
* everytime you upload a new version, platforms will verify if it's you or someone else.
* that is why apk sign is very important.
